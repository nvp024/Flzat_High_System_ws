Use these three fields:
{"speech":"","goal":null,"interaction":null}

speech: the spoken reply, or "" when no reply is useful.

goal is null or exactly:
{"description":"natural-language objective","constraints":[],"selected_context_refs":[],"missing_information":[]}

interaction is null or exactly:
{"kind":"ASK","question":"one concise question?"}

description and question are nonempty strings. The three goal arrays contain
strings; use [] when empty. Copy selected_context_refs exactly from the input.
Put known requirements in constraints and unresolved details in missing_information.
When asking, set goal to null and speech to ""; interaction.question is the
complete spoken question, on one line ending in "?". Otherwise interaction is null.
For speech only, goal is also null. At least one field must carry content.
